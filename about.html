About Us

Village Lifestyle Pub is a proudly village-based establishment located in the heart of Limpopo. As the first pub-restaurant in the area, we pioneered local nightlife and created a space where people could gather, drink, connect, and enjoy themselves without leaving the village.

Our focus has always been on alcohol, music, and social vibes - the real village lifestyle. Over the years, we’ve grown into a trusted hangout for locals and visitors looking for a relaxed but lively atmosphere. Whether it’s after a long week, a celebration, or just a good time with friends, Village Lifestyle Pub is where the village comes alive. Located at 1014 Kings St, Taaiboschgroet, Limpopo, The Village Lifestyle is a local bar & grill known for its friendly atmosphere, music, and community spirit.

We’re open every day for lunch, dinner and late-night hangouts on weekends. Whether you’re stopping by for a casual drink or celebrating with friends, you’re always welcome.
